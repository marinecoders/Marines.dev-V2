title: "June 2024: First Marine Coders Sprint at MCSWF"
description: "A seven-day sprint brought twenty-five personnel from the Marine Innovation Unit (MIU) and Marine Corps Tactical Systems Support Activity (MCTSSA) to the Marine Corps Software Factory (MCSWF) in Austin, Texas. The event was designed to boost the Marine Corps' software development capabilities through a two-phase evolution. The initial phase involved comprehensive Tactical Assault Kit (TAK) training led by MCTSSA, aiming to build capacity and competency in using TAK applications at the tactical edge, a growing emphasis across the Department of Defense. Following the training, the participants engaged in Phase 2, a four-day hackathon that focused on three specific software projects requested by the MCSWF, along with a collaborative strategy session involving all three units to align future development efforts."
