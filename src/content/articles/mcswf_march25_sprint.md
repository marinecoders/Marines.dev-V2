title: "March 2025 Marine Coders Sprint at MCSWF"
description: "A fourteen-day product sprint, held from March 16-29, 2025, gathered thirty-six personnel from the Marine Innovation Unit (MIU) and the Marine Corps Tactical Systems Support Activity (MCTSSA) at the Marine Corps Software Factory (MCSWF) in Austin, Texas. The team, comprising twenty-nine MIU Marines and seven MCTSSA Marines, focused exclusively on collaborative software development across three key product lines. The sprint's objective was to accelerate progress on several critical applications, including the ARES HLZ and Flight Mode Plugins, the Reserve Hub web application, and CRUSADER, delivering tangible advancements for use across the Fleet Marine Force."
