title: "September 2025 Marine Coders Sprint at MCSWF"
description: "Fourteen MIU Marine Coders participated conducted a week-long sprint at the Marine Corps Software Factory in Austin, Texas, where they rapidly developed and refined applications to enhance operational capabilities. Leveraging their civilian expertise, the coders collaborated on several high-impact, warfighter-focused projects. Key initiatives included refining the ARES HLZ (Augmented Reconnaissance and Estimate of the Situation) application for identifying safe helicopter landing zones, and developing a Flight Mode TAK plugin to help pilots more easily receive and record digital information in the cockpit. The team also contributed to the Reserve Hub website and the CRUSADER application, a government-built software designed to control commercial radar systems more effectively for tactical use. This initiative highlighted the Marine Corps' commitment to leveraging organic software development talent to address real-world challenges at the speed of operations."
